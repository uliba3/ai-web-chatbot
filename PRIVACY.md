# Privacy Policy for AI Web Chatbot Chrome Extension

Last updated: 2024

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Web Chatbot Chrome Extension ("we", "our", or "us") handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Data Collection and Usage

### What We Collect

1. **Web Page Content**: The extension analyzes the content of web pages you visit to provide contextual AI responses. This content is processed locally in your browser and is not stored permanently.

2. **Chat History**: Your conversation history with the AI is stored locally in your browser's storage and is not transmitted to any external servers.

3. **Extension Settings**: Any preferences or settings you configure within the extension are stored locally in your browser.

### What We Don't Collect

- Personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
- Browsing history
- Login credentials
- Payment information
- Any other sensitive personal data

## Data Storage

All data collected by the extension is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. We do not maintain any external servers or databases to store your information.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties. All processing is done locally within your browser.

## Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your locally stored data
- Clear your chat history and settings
- Uninstall the extension at any time

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the GitHub repository issues section.

## Consent
